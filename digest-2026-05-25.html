SM2-Inspired Grimdark NMM — Dark Metallic Blends Referencing In-Game Aesthetic
YouTube Instagram Reddit
The Space Marine 2 visual aesthetic — dark, gritty, almost monochrome metallic Space Marines with heavy contrast and dark gold trim — is driving a wave of Non-Metallic Metal (NMM) and dark metallic paint technique tutorials. Creators are replicating the in-game look on tabletop models, fuelled by the Purgation update's renewed attention on the game.
Organic Full context ↓

Space Marine 2's rendering engine produces a distinctive visual style: heavy ambient occlusion, dark primary colors (Ultramarine blue reads almost navy-black in most lighting), bright specular highlights, and rich dark gold trim on armor details. This is influencing tabletop painters who want their models to look "like the game." The technique approach: mix Incubi Darkness/Kantor Blue deep base instead of straight Macragge Blue, heavy Nuln Oil in recesses, Calgar Blue/Fenrisian for highlights pushed harder toward pure white on extreme edges. For the NMM gold: Rhinox Hide base, Doombull Brown mid, Skullcrusher Brass to Liberator Gold for specular, with Agrax and targeted Brown Ink for shadow. Tutorial videos using SM2 screenshots as reference are performing well. The Iron Hands DLC release this week adds a new colorway to this trend (bare metal + dark iron recipes).

The format borrows from car reveal and fashion content and is spreading rapidly through the 40k creator community.
Organic Full context ↓

The "countdown zoom army reveal" is a defined short-form content format: typically 15–45 seconds, starts on a detail shot (a single model's face, a weapon, a basing element), slowly reveals the full unit/army across 3–5 cuts with cinematic or dramatic music, often ending on a wide army shot or a title card with army name/size. The format is being widely replicated because it performs well algorithmically (hook in the first 2 seconds via the close-up, sustained engagement via the reveal structure) and requires no voiceover or editing skill beyond basic cuts. The SM2 OST and DOW4 trailer audio are the dominant audio choices for this format. Edition-hype timing has boosted it — creators want to show off completed armies "before the new edition drops." The technique requires good in-focus macro photography skills, which is creating secondary tutorials on "how to film your minis for TikTok."
